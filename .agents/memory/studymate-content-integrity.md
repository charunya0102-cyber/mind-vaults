---
name: StudyMate content integrity
description: Scope guard for future visual redesigns of StudyMate.
---

Treat StudyMate redesign requests as presentation-only. Preserve existing subjects, lessons, topics, notes, flashcards, quizzes, timetable, exams, tasks, study sessions, planner data, progress, saved information, and functionality. Never reset or seed the student's academic workspace with sample content.

**Why:** The user has repeated that redesigns must not disturb their existing study records or introduce sample academic content.

**How to apply:** Before changing StudyMate, keep edits to display markup and styling unless the user explicitly authorizes broader changes; do not touch persistence or existing academic records.
